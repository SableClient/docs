Sable allows you to create and responde to polls that are compliant with the spec and allow you to interact with polls made by anyone.

# Creation

You can create a poll by either selecting the + button on the left of the editor, and then pressing the Create Poll option in the menu, or by typing the command /poll to bring up the poll modal.

The poll creation screen allows you to have as many responses as your heart desires.

You can select whether you want the people to see the answers as the poll is ongoing or not.

You can have as many answers to a poll as you want, most other clients that have implemented do not allow implement for the creation, and have limited support on them.

# The poll message

New message containing the poll allows you to answer straight from it, and to end it if you have the ability.

### Once you have casted an answer you can see:
  - The number of votes an aswer has as (x votes)
  - The percentage of the votes that go to that answer in a bar under the option, which when hovered shows you a percentage of the people that have voted for it
  - Which people have voted for each specific option by clicking on the (x votes) text to the right of the option

### Additionally, at the bottom of the message you can see:
  - The total number of votes and of voters to the poll (in case that these numbers are not equal)
  - How many option a voter might choose (in case that is different from either 1 option or as many as the total number of options)
  - Whether a poll will show you the answer as the votes are being casted
