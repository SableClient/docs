Matrix is a chat network. Different apps can talk to each other across different
servers. Sable is one of those apps. The server that holds your account is your
**homeserver**.

## Your account

Your Matrix ID looks like `@name:example.org`. The part after the colon is your
homeserver. That server owns your login. Locked out? Ask whoever runs it.

## Rooms and spaces

- A **room** is a chat you join with a link or address.
- A **space** groups related rooms together.
- A **direct message** is just a private room between you and someone else.

## Encrypted messages

In encrypted rooms, only the devices in the room can read the messages. Save
your recovery key when Sable offers it, and verify new devices when asked. That
keeps you able to read old history later.

## Choose a homeserver

Your homeserver stores your account, room history, and the rules for sign-up and
moderation. Pick one you trust. You can move later, but it is annoying, so plan
ahead if you can.

More background: [Matrix chat basics](https://matrix.org/docs/chat_basics/matrix-for-im/).
